Meet the Coach

Grace Stewart

Grace Stewart is a former championship player and one of the most accomplished volleyball coaches in the Christian County area. With over 12 years of experience spanning competitive play, head coaching, camp directing, and private instruction, Grace brings a deep understanding of what it takes to develop great players at every level.

"My focus is on the whole athlete — strengthening skills and the mind for big moment performance. I believe in promoting positive attitude and leadership qualities. Your athlete will learn what coaches are looking for and how to stand out from every other player on the court."

— Coach Grace Stewart · EliteVB · Hopkinsville, KY

📋 How to book
1. Click the duration dropdown at the top of the calendar (e.g. "1 hr"). The options appear below the calendar — scroll down to pick one.
2. Choose an available day (bold/clickable days have open slots), then pick a time.
3. In the notes box, tell Coach Grace which type of session you want:
For a private lesson, write:
"Private lesson — [player's name]"

For a small group, write:
"Small group — [list every player attending]"
One person books the slot for the whole group. Please list everyone's names.
